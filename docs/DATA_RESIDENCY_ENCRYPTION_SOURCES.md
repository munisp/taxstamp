# Data Residency and Encryption Assessment Sources

## Nigeria Data Protection Commission

The NDPC’s official **Nigeria Data Protection Act General Application and Implementation Directive 2025** is the operative guidance reviewed for this assessment. Its table of contents includes general compliance measures, major-importance controller/processor classification, DPO designation, DPIAs, data-processing agreements, information security measures, breach notification, data-subject rights, and cross-border data transfers. The directive supports a risk-based analysis of data location, transfer governance, technical/organisational safeguards, processor contracts and evidence rather than a repository-only declaration of compliance.

Source: [NDPC GAID 2025](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf).

## Central Bank of Nigeria

The CBN’s official Payments System Supervision page states its mandate includes payment-system soundness and safety through rules and guidelines, promotion of strong internal controls, transparency and accountability in fintechs, effective supervision, and monitoring with early-warning capability. The page describes availability without interruption and minimum risk as national payment-system objectives. It does not, on its own, specify a blanket data-localisation or a particular encryption algorithm requirement; licence-specific, outsourcing, cloud, cyber-risk and payment-scheme obligations must therefore be confirmed with the relevant CBN framework/circular and Nigerian counsel.

Source: [CBN Payments System Supervision](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/PaymentsSystem/).

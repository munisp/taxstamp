## Cross-channel conclusion

The API/control-plane core, Expo foundation, and separate PWA have independent local evidence. Their statuses must not be transferred between channels. In particular, a passing PWA build does not prove mobile device security; a passing Expo typecheck does not prove API authorization; a local APISIX denial does not prove a real Keycloak/Permify authorization decision; and a local ledger test does not prove a live TigerBeetle, Mojaloop, bank, or regulator effect.
